## 📚 Study Strategy

### Daily Routine (30-45 minutes)
1. **Review Due Cards** (15 min): Use spaced repetition on flashcards
2. **Learn New Topic** (20 min): Add 3-5 new questions
3. **Practice Verbal Response** (10 min): Answer questions out loud

### Weekly Goals
- [ ] Master 20-30 questions per week
- [ ] Complete 2-3 scenario walkthroughs
- [ ] Review and update confidence ratings
- [ ] Add questions from mock interviews

### Before Each Interview
- [ ] Review company-specific questions in [[06_Companies/|Companies folder]]
- [ ] Practice top 10 questions for the role
- [ ] Prepare behavioral STAR examples
- [ ] Review recent interview logs for patterns
