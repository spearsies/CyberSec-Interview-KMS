# ❓ Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a teammate.

## ✅ Quick Answer
Pick a real, resolved conflict. Show that you separated the problem from the person, listened for the underlying concern, found common ground on a shared goal, and reached a constructive outcome — then say what you learned. Focus on *your* behavior, never on blaming the other person.

## 🗣️ Talking points
- Structure it with STAR.
- Describe the disagreement objectively and without heat.
- Emphasize listening and data over ego.
- Reframe around the shared goal to break the deadlock.
- Resolution preserved the working relationship; close with the lesson.

## ⚠️ Follow-ups they'll hit you with
- What if the conflict *wasn't* resolved?
- How do you disagree with your manager?
