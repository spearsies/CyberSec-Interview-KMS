# ❓ Compare the NIST and SANS incident response processes.

## ✅ Quick Answer
Both describe the same lifecycle with different groupings. **NIST SP 800-61** has 4 phases: Preparation; Detection & Analysis; Containment, Eradication & Recovery; Post-Incident Activity. **SANS** uses 6 (PICERL): Preparation, Identification, Containment, Eradication, Recovery, Lessons Learned. SANS simply splits NIST's combined middle phase out — pick one and stay consistent.

## 🗣️ Talking points
- Map PICERL onto NIST's four phases.
- Both are iterative, not strictly linear — you loop back as you learn.
- Preparation and Lessons Learned bookend the process and drive improvement.
- Know which framework your org has adopted.
- Interviewers want the phases and *actions*, not rote labels.

## ⚠️ Follow-ups they'll hit you with
- Which do you prefer and why?
- What actually happens in Lessons Learned?
