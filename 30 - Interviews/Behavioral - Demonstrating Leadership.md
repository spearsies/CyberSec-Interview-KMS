# ❓ Tell me about a time you showed leadership, with or without authority.

## ✅ Quick Answer
At the senior-analyst level, leadership is usually **influence without authority**: you spotted a gap, took ownership, brought others along, and drove an outcome. Show the initiative, how you motivated and coordinated people, and the measurable result — mentoring a junior, driving a process fix, or running an incident bridge.

## 🗣️ Talking points
- Influence without a title — the senior differentiator.
- Concrete initiative: built a runbook, led a hunt, mentored an analyst.
- How you rallied and coordinated others.
- Measurable result and team-level (not just personal) impact.
- Frame it as improving the team/process, not solo heroics.

## ⚠️ Follow-ups they'll hit you with
- Give an example of leading without authority.
- Tell me about a time you led and it failed.
