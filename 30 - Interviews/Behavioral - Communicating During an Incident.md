# ❓ How do you communicate during a major incident, including to non-technical stakeholders?

## ✅ Quick Answer
Communicate clearly, calmly, and on a cadence. Designate a single source of truth, translate technical facts into business impact for leadership, give regular updates even when there's no news, and keep an incident timeline. Match the message to the audience — analysts get IOCs, executives get impact and ETA.

## 🗣️ Talking points
- Single comms lead / source of truth to avoid conflicting messages.
- Audience-tailored: technical detail for responders, impact for execs.
- Regular update cadence, even "no change yet."
- State impact, actions taken, next steps — avoid speculation.
- Maintain a timeline for the post-incident review; manage up without over-promising.

## ⚠️ Follow-ups they'll hit you with
- How do you update an exec who wants an answer you don't have yet?
- Who should own external/customer communications?
