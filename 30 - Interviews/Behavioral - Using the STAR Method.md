# ❓ How do you structure a behavioral interview answer?

## ✅ Quick Answer
Use **STAR**: Situation (brief context), Task (your responsibility), Action (what *you* did — the bulk of the answer), Result (a quantified outcome plus the lesson). Keep it concise, own your specific actions ("I," not "we"), and land on a measurable result.

## 🗣️ Talking points
- Situation and Task stay short; Action is the longest part; Result is quantified.
- Say "I" — interviewers want *your* contribution.
- Pick real, relevant stories tied to the role.
- Prepare 5–6 flexible stories (conflict, failure, leadership, pressure, ambiguity).
- End every answer with impact + what you learned.

## ⚠️ Follow-ups they'll hit you with
- What if you don't have a perfect example?
- How long should a STAR answer be?
