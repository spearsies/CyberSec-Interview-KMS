# ❓ Why does data classification matter and how is it used operationally?

## ✅ Quick Answer
Classification labels data by sensitivity — e.g. Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted — so controls, handling, and monitoring scale to risk. It drives encryption, access, retention, DLP policy, and incident severity: you can't protect or prioritize what you haven't classified.

## 🗣️ Talking points
- Typical tiers and what each permits for storage, transit, and sharing.
- Drives control selection: encryption, access model, retention, DLP rules.
- Feeds exfiltration detection and alert prioritization (crown-jewel data raises severity).
- Regulatory tie-in: PII, PHI, PCI carry mandated handling.
- Automated labeling (e.g. Microsoft Purview sensitivity labels) enforces handling at scale.

## ⚠️ Follow-ups they'll hit you with
- How does classification change incident severity?
- Who is responsible for classifying data?
